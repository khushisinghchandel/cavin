About Cavin Pharma

Affordable medicine with innovation.

Who we are

We are a company that takes on the toughest health challenges. But we know there is more to do than treat disease: we aim to make a remarkable impact on people’s lives.

Cavin is a multi-location organization with a rapidly growing presence in the pharmaceutical industry, across both acute and chronic segments.

We maintain high quality standards at every stage of the manufacturing process, delivering quality medicines at affordable prices through WHO, GMP and ISO certified manufacturing partners.

Cavin believes that quality is a continuous process and not an isolated stage.
